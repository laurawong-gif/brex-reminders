How to use this tool
  1. Make sure you're signed in. The top bar should show "✅ Signed in as <your @masterclass.com address>". If not, click Sign in with Google and sign in with your work account.
  2. Download the blank XLSX template (link in the subtitle above), then populate it with this month's outstanding Brex ER data from Coupa.
  3. Drop the XLSX file onto the upload zone above (or click "browse to upload").
  4. Optional: click anywhere in a draft preview to tweak the wording for that specific person. An "EDITED" badge appears, and a "Reset edits" button lets you revert.
  5. Click "Create all drafts in Gmail" at the top of the drafts list. All drafts will appear in your Gmail Drafts folder — fully formatted, in just a few seconds.
  6. Open Gmail Drafts. For each draft: switch the From dropdown to procurement@masterclass.com (or set it as your default Send-mail-as), insert your Gmail signature, and click Send.

Footnote — if you can't sign in to Google: use the "Open in Gmail compose" button on each draft instead. It opens a Gmail compose tab with To/Subject pre-filled AND copies the formatted email body to your clipboard. In the compose tab, click in the body area, paste (Cmd+V / Ctrl+V), switch the From dropdown, insert your signature, and Send. This fallback works without OAuth but requires one extra paste per draft.
